Door Tolerances

We consider warping, twisting, cupping, and bowing of doors to be defects only if they exceed the following tolerances:

- Warping: 6mm in the plane of the door.
- Twist/Cup: 2mm across the width of the door.
- Bow: 4mm along the length of the door.
